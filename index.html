Our story

A single grill, a long line, and a burger worth waiting for.

Big Wave started as a twelve-seat counter behind the harbour with one flat-top and a hand-written menu. We still grind our beef twice daily, still bake the brioche before we open, and still think a burger should be the thing you drive across town for.

A decade on, the line got longer and the menu grew — pizza from the wood oven, foot-long subs, sea-salt fries and shakes thick enough to stand a spoon in. The burgers never changed. They never will.
